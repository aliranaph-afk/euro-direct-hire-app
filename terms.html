3. Plans, prices, and access

The displayed one-time plans are Bronze (₱39, up to 7 searches per day for 7 days), Silver (₱49, up to 14 searches per day for 14 days), and Gold (₱59, up to 21 searches per day for 21 days). New paid checkout is limited to 200 occupied paid slots; free-tier access is not included in that limit. Active paid subscribers and applicants whose payment references have been accepted and are awaiting manual review count toward capacity. Selecting a plan does not reserve a slot. The site checks availability when a plan is selected and hides the QR when capacity is full. If a user has already seen the QR and completes a transfer while capacity changes, the system still accepts their submitted reference for manual review and may approve and activate the selected tier even if that temporarily takes occupied slots above 200. While capacity is at or above 200, no new paid checkout is offered; checkout reopens when occupied slots fall below 200. Access starts only after payment verification and activation by the Operator. The plan duration is counted from activation. Plans do not automatically renew.
