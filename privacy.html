Data privacy

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who is responsible for your information?

EuroDirect-Hire is operated by Rana Trading, based in South Caloocan, Philippines (the “Operator”). The complete registered business address has not been provided. For privacy requests, contact ali.ranaph@gmail.com and ask for the person responsible for privacy matters. This contact is not identified here as a Data Protection Officer.

Information we handle

  • Your email address and one-time verification code when you request account access.
  • Job title and destination queries, and account/search usage needed to return listings and apply plan limits.
  • If payment submissions are enabled: selected plan, email address, GCash transaction reference, and the name you provide for manual payment verification. Do not send PINs, passwords, full card details, or other sensitive information.
  • Basic technical information may be processed by the site host and service providers to operate and secure the site; their own retention practices may apply.

If you choose a paid plan after paid capacity is confirmed, the checkout displays a GCash/InstaPay QR and the payee name supplied by the Operator. Your name and payment reference are collected only when you submit them for manual verification; if approved, the selected tier is activated and confirmation is sent to your account email.

Why we use it

Information is used to authenticate access, provide job searches, apply usage limits, respond to support requests, verify payments if enabled, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. The Operator should document the applicable lawful basis for each purpose under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and its implementing rules; processing is not based on consent merely because this notice is displayed.

Service providers and international processing

We may use third-party service providers to host the site and support sign-in, email delivery, search, storage, and site styling. These providers may process information needed to provide those services and may retain their own technical logs or backups under their own practices. The Operator has not confirmed each provider’s retention periods or processing locations, so the 30-day deletion target below does not promise deletion from provider systems. Job listing links lead to third-party websites governed by their own privacy notices.

Retention and security

After the Apps Script backend is deployed and its cleanup trigger is installed, payment-submission rows that are at least 30 days old are scheduled for deletion. User account rows at least 30 days old are deleted when the account has neither an active paid plan nor a recent pending payment; account data is retained while needed to complete a pending payment or provide an active paid plan and becomes eligible for deletion after those needs end. The cleanup checks every six hours, so deletion occurs on a cleanup run after the 30-day mark, not necessarily at the exact moment it is reached. One-time verification codes expire after 10 minutes. This does not delete copies held in provider logs or backups, which may follow separate schedules. No internet transmission or storage method can be promised to be completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, request erasure or blocking, or exercise other rights available under Philippine privacy law. Contact ali.ranaph@gmail.com and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity and explain any legal reason we cannot fully comply.

Changes and privacy concerns

We may update this notice and will revise the date above. For questions or concerns, email ali.ranaph@gmail.com. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission through its official channels.
